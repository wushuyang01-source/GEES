EMS (Energy Management System)

Smart Control for Electric & Hybrid Vessels

The PLC-based Energy Management System ensures reliable power distribution, optimized energy usage, and intelligent hybrid operation modes.

EFFICIENT FLEXIBLE

EMS Overview

The PLC-based EMS is a critical control system for electric and hybrid vessels. It collects and analyzes data from propulsion motors, generators, and inverters, while seamlessly interfacing with the Battery Management System (BMS). Through intuitive visualization, the EMS provides operators with real-time monitoring of all system parameters. It dynamically controls power flows, ensuring batteries are charged and discharged optimally, and hybrid motors can flexibly switch between motor and generator modes.

Benefits / 用户价值

Beyond power control, EMS enhances overall vessel efficiency, reduces fuel consumption in hybrid modes, and extends battery lifetime through optimized charge cycles. It reduces operator workload with automated decision-making, while maintaining full manual override capability. EMS provides long-term lifecycle value by lowering operating costs and simplifying future retrofits.
